# 0bull

[0bull](https://0bull.net) rents real iPhones by the month. This plugin connects Claude to the phones on your 0bull account so it can look at a phone's screen, read its text, tap, swipe and type, send device commands, replay your recorded macros, and hand narrow tasks to the on-phone agent, all on actual hardware rather than a simulator.

## What you need

- A 0bull account with at least one phone assigned.
- Sign in once: run `/mcp`, choose the `0bull` server and complete the 0bull login in your browser. The plugin only reaches the phones on that account.

## What it does

The `iphone-control` skill teaches Claude to look before it acts, follow queued work until it finishes, and ask you before anything destructive (a reboot, clearing photos, sending messages, purchases). It never types passwords, passcodes or verification codes: when a login or code screen appears, it stops and asks you to complete it on the phone. It does not post to social platforms.

Try: "Show me my 0bull phones and what is on the first screen."
